## Operations Lab interaction

The Lab has five views: Flow, Transport, Proof, Recovery, and Closure. One tab row changes the visible projection. A scenario selector changes only the read-only Recovery Simulation. The Lab cannot execute commands, edit metadata, reorder questions, or expose interview content.

Each view remains usable at desktop, 320 CSS pixels, 280 CSS pixels, and print. Text remains the primary encoding; compact bars and rails are supplementary and accessible by labels.

## Error and privacy behavior

- Invalid, missing, stale, or future-dated evidence yields `unknown`, `stale`, or `resync_required`, never a healthy assumption.
- Repeated rendered text cannot be reused as proof after its record is consumed.
- Commit and delivery leases fail closed on generation or expected-owner mismatch.
- Operations Lab projections use counts, identifiers, reason codes, durations, states, and fingerprints only.
- Question text, answer text, setup context, clipboard data, credentials, tokens, cookies, and raw URLs remain excluded.
